For Flick Sakes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026

For Flick Sakes is a television and film discovery application that helps users search for titles, receive recommendations, save preferences and discover where content may be available to watch.

Information we process

For Flick Sakes does not require users to create an account and does not request a user's name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone or payment information through the app.

The app may store preferences, watchlist information and personalisation settings locally on the user's device.

Information sent to our services

When users perform searches, request recommendations or check streaming availability, the app communicates with For Flick Sakes backend services. Requests may include search text, recommendation preferences, title identifiers and the selected country or streaming region.

Third-party services

For Flick Sakes uses third-party services to provide television and film catalogue information and streaming availability information. Information necessary to fulfil a request may be transmitted to these providers.

Advertising

For Flick Sakes does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use user information for targeted advertising.

External links

The app may provide links to third-party streaming services or websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed through the service. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention

Locally stored preferences remain on the user's device until they are changed, reset or the app is removed. Server-side operational information may be retained only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure and improve the service.

Children

For Flick Sakes is not specifically directed at children and is intended for a general entertainment audience.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app and its services evolve. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy or support enquiries, contact Support@forflicksakes.com.
